About us

Bigfirebeats.com was created to help upcoming artists and content creators release their music or projects without any stress about buying our beats first before releasing.For upcoming artists they will have to buy the beat later after their song starts getting recognition.In this process they must first agree to our terms of use before using our beats.


Our goal is to help upcoming artists release their music, focus on improving their quality and gaining more fans from their songs without needing money to pay the producer for using their beats before releasing their songs.They must first test how people would react to their songs and buy the beat later after getting their royalties from the music they made.They will have to pay a once off fee which is $7.99 to whitelist their youtube, Instagram and Facebook accounts , so that they can use all our free beats and gain royalties from those projects.And when the song reaches over 100 thousand streams they will then have to buy the beat later.
